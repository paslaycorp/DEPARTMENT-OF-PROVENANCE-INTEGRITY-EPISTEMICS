# Genesis 2.0 vs. AEIF

## Purpose

This document records a provenance-first comparison between publicly described Genesis 2.0 material and the Alexandrian Epistemic Integrity Framework (AEIF).

It is a comparative record, not an accusation of copying and not a determination of authorship, priority, derivation, or infringement.

The governing sequence is:

**similarity → correspondence → evidence → chronology → provenance**

Technical similarity alone does not establish copying, derivation, or priority.

## Scope

The comparison is limited to propositions that can be tied to identifiable public statements, specifications, implementations, repository artifacts, commits, or other evidence.

The current Genesis evidence set consists of publicly accessible LinkedIn posts by Carl Bousquet located during the 2026-08-28 evidence review. These posts are useful primary public statements of what Genesis 2.0 is claimed to do, but they are not substitutes for the underlying Genesis source artifacts or a versioned technical specification.

Where an underlying artifact, exact version, publication date, hash, or implementation cannot presently be established, that field remains **PENDING** or **UNRESOLVED**.

## What the comparison establishes now

The current evidence establishes that there are **meaningful conceptual and functional correspondences** between publicly described Genesis 2.0 mechanisms and several AEIF concerns, particularly around state transition, coherence/stability, verification, governance, auditability, constrained action, and cross-model behavior.

It does **not** establish that the systems are the same architecture, that one was derived from the other, or that either party copied the other.

Several apparent correspondences may arise independently because the systems address overlapping classes of problems. Stronger conclusions require artifact-level comparison and chronology.

## Chronology

AEIF repository chronology is directly inspectable from this repository beginning with its dated commits. The repository's own provenance rules correctly distinguish repository publication from earlier creation or conception.

For Genesis 2.0, the public posts located during this review provide dated public records at the page level, but the exact publication timestamps, version identifiers, and underlying technical artifacts still need to be captured as immutable evidence. The public material also contains retrospective statements about Genesis development reaching back to June 2025; those statements are recorded as **DOCUMENTED AS AUTHOR STATEMENTS**, not as independently verified creation dates.

Chronological priority must therefore remain unresolved until contemporaneous Genesis artifacts or equivalent independent evidence are captured.

## Evidence discipline

Every material comparison should ultimately resolve to the repository's canonical relation:

**Claim → Artifact → Version → Date → Evidence → Hash → Location**

The evidence register supplies the external evidence IDs. The comparison ledger records the propositions. Neither document may silently promote a missing field into an inferred fact.

### Prohibited inference

> technical similarity → copying

### Admissible progression

> similarity → correspondence → evidence → chronology → provenance

Even strong technical correspondence establishes only that two systems contain related mechanisms unless additional evidence establishes a stronger relationship.

## Important epistemic distinction

Genesis public posts frequently contain statements generated by or attributed to AI systems in response to Genesis prompts. Those outputs are evidence that the interaction occurred and that the model produced the reported characterization. They are **not, by themselves, independent verification of the underlying architectural claims**.

Accordingly, the record distinguishes:

- **Claim made by the author**
- **Observed model response**
- **Primary technical artifact**
- **Independent corroboration**
- **Derived interpretation**
- **Unresolved proposition**

This distinction is material to any future priority or derivation analysis.

## Current conclusion

**Status: DOCUMENTED / PARTIALLY UNRESOLVED**

The evidence currently supports a serious comparative investigation, not a final provenance conclusion.

The strongest next step is not rhetorical escalation. It is preservation of the underlying Genesis artifacts, exact versions, publication timestamps, hashes where possible, and contemporaneous chronology, followed by an artifact-to-artifact semantic comparison.
